About Blueline Construction

Blueline Construction is a family-owned business with over 30 years of experience in the construction industry. We pride ourselves on providing reliable equipment and exceptional customer service to help you get the job done right.

Who We Are

Blueline Construction started out supplying groundworks equipment to regional road contractors and has grown into a dedicated plant hire fleet for roads and bridges. We're built around one job: keeping heavy equipment maintained, available, and on site when a project needs it.

What We Do

We supply and maintain heavy plant for road and bridge construction, from excavators and rollers to mobile cranes and site power. Every machine is inspected and serviced between hires, with operators available for specialist lifting and paving work.
